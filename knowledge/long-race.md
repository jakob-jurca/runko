## Scenario: long_race
Who: training for a half marathon or longer (anything above 10 km, ultras included). Time on feet and durability decide the race.
How the plan is built (the app enforces these; explain them, never contradict them):
- The long run is central. It ramps from their current longest run (+2 km or 15% a week; +1 km / 10% for newer runners) to the goal's peak: ~18-21 km for a half, ~30-32 km for a marathon, max 35 km for ultras — but never longer than 2.5 h (3 h in marathon plans) at their easy pace, whichever comes first. A slower runner whose long run stops at the time cap is ready; say so. At the peak it alternates full and shorter (~85%) long runs.
- Classic periodization: base → build → sharpen → taper. Recovery week every 4th week (~75% volume).
- Quality supports the long run: threshold (tempo) sessions; experienced runners on 5+ days add intervals; sharpen adds goal/marathon-pace work (M ≤ 20% of the week).
- Taper: 2 weeks for a half, 3 for a marathon and longer; volume cut 40-60%, intensity kept.
- Minimum readiness: half ~25 km/week and a 15 km long run; marathon ~40 km/week, a 26 km long run and at least 3 run days. Below that the goal is judged unsafe and a safer one is built.
How to talk to them:
- Long runs at easy pace; goal-pace segments only late in the build.
- Practise race fuelling on long runs over 75-90 min (see nutrition.md).
- Missing one long run is fine; do not double up the next week.
- Race: even or slightly negative split; the first half should feel too easy.
