## Seasonal plan anchors (for plan_generation)
- Spring goal (March-May: Kraški, Istrski, Wings for Life, Tek trojk, Radenci): build through winter; base Dec-Jan; indoor/treadmill alternatives needed for icy/foggy days.
- Autumn goal (Ljubljana Marathon, October): build through summer; long runs early morning due to heat; taper starts late September/early October.
- Summer: fewer road races, more trail/mountain races; good base or trail season.

## Climate considerations (Ljubljana basin; ARSO normals)
- Continental climate. January mean ~0 °C (frequent sub-zero mornings); July mean ~20-21 °C, afternoon highs ~27-28 °C on average, heat waves 32-36 °C increasingly common.
- Precipitation ~1,300-1,400 mm/year; frequent summer thunderstorms (afternoon/evening).
- Autumn/winter fog and temperature inversions in the Ljubljana basin; winter air quality (PM10) can be poor on inversion days -> on high-pollution days, move quality sessions indoors or to higher ground; people with asthma should be careful.
- Summer: run early morning (before 8:00) or late evening; run by effort (see vdot.md heat adjustments); humidity is high in the basin. Marathon performance declines as temperature rises above ~10-12 °C. [Ely 2007]
- Winter: ice on paths -> reduce pace, choose cleared roads or treadmill; dress in layers, cover hands and ears; in darkness wear a headlamp and reflective gear (pedestrians on roads outside settlements at night must be visible with a reflector or light under Slovenian traffic rules).
- Coast (Primorska): milder winters; strong bora wind (burja) in Kras and coast can make pacing impossible, run by effort.
- Alps/mountains: rapid weather changes, afternoon storms in summer; carry layer and phone on mountain runs; altitude generally < 2,500 m, minor effect on pace except above ~1,500 m.

## Health and outdoor risks specific to Slovenia
- Ticks: Slovenia has one of the highest incidences of tick-borne encephalitis (klopni meningoencefalitis, KME) and Lyme borreliosis in Europe. Trail and forest runners: tick check after runs; vaccination against KME is recommended by NIJZ (discuss with doctor). Seek medical advice for fever or expanding red rash (erythema migrans) after a tick bite.
- Wildlife: brown bears present mainly in southern forests (Kočevje, Notranjska); on remote trail runs, make noise and avoid dawn/dusk solo runs there.
- Hunting season in forests (autumn): stay on marked trails; bright clothing.
- Emergency number: 112.
