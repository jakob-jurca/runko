Lydiard method (m04). Phases in order: conditioning (base), hills, anaerobic, coordination, freshening.
- Conditioning is long: at least 10 weeks; almost everything aerobic; no high-intensity work except a few strides. Runners at novice level and above add one steady run at 3/4 effort (up to 60 minutes or 15% of weekly time).
- Beginners: all runs easy, no steady runs for six weeks; start with 15 minutes a day, every third day 30 minutes, raising continuous time by at most 10 minutes a week up to 60.
- Long run about 25% of weekly time, 45-150 minutes (180 for a marathon), one per week in every phase.
- Hills: 3 sessions a week for advanced, 2 intermediate, 1 novice, none for beginners (strides uphill instead); grades 4-10%.
- With fewer than 12 weeks to the race the full sequence is not used.
