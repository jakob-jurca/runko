Altitude (s06).
- Below 1000 m: no change. From 1000 m go by heart rate and RPE; pace is only a reference.
- Expected slowdown at threshold and race pace: 1000-1500 m 1-2%, 1500-2000 m 2-4%, 2000-2500 m 4-6%, 2500-3000 m 6-9%.
- First days: from 1500 m with up to 2 days at altitude, easy running only; from 2000 m with up to 3 days, easy running only and the long run 20% shorter.
- Fast intervals at altitude: recoveries 1.5x longer (2x from 2000 m) and reps a fifth fewer. From 2500 m no fast intervals: go lower to do them.
- Headache, nausea, breathlessness at rest: descend and see a doctor.
