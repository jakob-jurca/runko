Hal Higdon plans (m07): simple, level-based plans.
- Beginner who cannot run 20 minutes non-stop: run-walk first.
- Novice 5K: 8 weeks, 3 run days (2.4-4.8 km), 3 walk days, 1 rest. Novice 10K: 8 weeks, 3 run days, 2 cross days, long run 4.8-8.9 km.
- Half: Novice 1 (12 weeks, 4 run days, long run 6.4-16 km, a strength day) for runners who can run 4.8 km 3-4 times a week; Intermediate 1 (12 weeks, 5 run days, long run to 19 km, about 48 km/week peak) from 25 km/week.
- First marathon: Novice 1 from 24 km/week on 4+ days; Novice 2 after a year of running at 24-40 km/week. Intermediate 1 after a marathon (35+ km/week, 5+ days); Advanced 1 after two marathons with speedwork (45+ km/week, 6+ days).
