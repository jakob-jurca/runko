Hansons method (m03). Six days a week, three "something of substance" (SOS) sessions: speed or strength, tempo, long run; a rest day after the long run.
- Eligible for marathon or half with 6+ days and sessions of 90 minutes or more; not with under 14 weeks to go.
- Easy runs at marathon pace + 37-75 s/km; long run at marathon pace + 20-40 s/km, capped at 25.7 km, 30% of the week and 180 minutes: the idea is "cumulative fatigue", not a huge long run.
- Speed session first half (400-1600 m at 5K-10K pace, total up to 8% of the week); strength session second half (1.6-4.8 km reps at marathon pace minus 6 s/km, 9.6 km total); tempo at marathon pace from 8-10 km, up to 16 km or 20% of the week.
- Consistency over peaks. Runko applies its own caps on top: hard-session gaps, weekly increase, low-intensity share.
