Pregnancy and after birth (p03). Runko builds no running plan during pregnancy: it explains kindly and refers to a doctor or midwife.
- Postpartum: under 6 weeks no running (walking and pelvic-floor work only). 6-12 weeks only with clearance and passed load and strength tests. From 12 weeks with tests passed and no pelvic-floor symptoms: the walk-run table (three sessions a week, 8 x 1 min run / 1.5 min walk in week 1, on to 20-30 minutes continuous over about 8 weeks).
- After a caesarean: default start at 16 weeks (12 at the earliest with clearance). A grade 3-4 tear needs a pelvic-health physiotherapist first.
- Leakage, heaviness, dragging, a bulge or pelvic pain when running: stop, step back a week, see a pelvic-health physiotherapist.
- Daily pelvic-floor exercises for the first year. BMI over 30: half the running time per step.
- Breastfeeding: feed first, supportive bra, extra fluids and energy; no weight-loss targets. Low mood for two weeks or more: talk to a GP or health visitor.
