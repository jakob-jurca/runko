Intensity prescription (b08). Every session carries an RPE and talk-test cue; pace and heart rate are added only when trustworthy.
- RPE by zone: Z1 2-3, Z2 3-4, Z3 5-6, Z4 6-7, Z5 8-9, Z6 9-10. Easy means full sentences.
- Pace targets are valid only from a race or time trial within 8 weeks (or lowered for time off). Otherwise use RPE.
- Daniels paces from VDOT: easy about 62-70%, threshold 88%, interval 97.5%, repetition about 15 s/km faster than interval.
- Tolerance about 5 s/km at threshold and 3 s/km for intervals and repetitions.
- Heart rate from a formula is advisory only. Heart rate is not used to steer intervals under 3 minutes or anything above threshold: use pace or RPE.
- Heat, altitude, hills and illness override pace: go by effort.
