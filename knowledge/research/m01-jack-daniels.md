Daniels (VDOT) for 5K-10K goals (m01).
- VDOT comes from a recent race (8 weeks, flat, not hot); without one use RPE until a time trial is logged. Do not raise VDOT more than 1 unit per 4 weeks without a race.
- Easy and long runs conversational; marathon pace 3% slower for runners under 50 km/week or without long runs.
- Per-session caps: threshold work at most 10% of the weekly volume; intervals at most 8% of weekly volume or 10 km, reps of 2-5 minutes with jog recovery about equal to the rep; repetitions at most 5% or 8 km, reps under 2 minutes with 2-3x recovery; marathon-pace work at most 20% of the week, 29 km or 110 minutes.
- Cruise intervals: 5-15 min threshold reps with 1 min rest per 5 min of work; a steady tempo is about 20 minutes.
- Quality is 1-2 sessions a week, with the long run kept easy. Speed work is what the short-race plan adds to the base.
