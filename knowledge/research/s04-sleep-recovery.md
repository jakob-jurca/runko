Sleep and recovery (s04).
- Target: 8 hours for adults, 9 for under 18; 8.5-9 in a heavy block.
- Under 6 hours the night before a hard session: shorten the main set by 30% or move it a day. Under 7 hours on 3 of 5 nights: cut weekly volume by 10% and allow one quality session at most.
- Under 18 with under 8 hours a night: show an injury-risk message and cap weekly increase at 5%.
- Early-morning quality on short sleep: move it later if possible. Nap 20-30 minutes before 15:00.
- Taper week: add 30-60 minutes in bed for 5-7 nights.
- Fragmented sleep with a healthy total (new parents) is not a reason for concern; the 24-hour total counts.
