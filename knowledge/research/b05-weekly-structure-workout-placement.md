Week structure and placement (b05).
- Quality sessions per week by level (base/build/peak): none 0/0/0; beginner 0/1/1; novice 1/1/1; intermediate 1/2/2; advanced 1/3/3. From age 50 at most 1/2/2 with the long run counted; 70+ at most 1; teens at most 2; on 3 run days 1-2, on 2 run days 0-1.
- Hard sessions need a gap: 48 h by default, 60 h at 50-59, 72 h at 60+. Beginners, novices never run hard on consecutive days.
- Long run on the runner's preferred day, else Sunday. Easy runs are at least 20 minutes and at most 0.75 of the long run.
- At least 75% of running time is easy (70% on three runs or fewer); if the week is over, the lowest-priority quality session becomes easy.
- Strides: 6-8 x 15-20 s on 1-2 easy days a week once the base is under way.
- Race week: the last quality session at least 4 days before a 5K/10K, 6 days before a half or marathon.
- Strength: two sessions a week in base, one in build and peak (two from 40), never within 48 h before a hard session, last heavy session a week before the race.
