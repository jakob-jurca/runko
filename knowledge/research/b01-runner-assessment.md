Runner assessment (runko-research b01).
- Experience level is the MEDIAN of three levels: years running, weekly volume (<10, 10-25, 25-50, 50-90, 90+ km) and longest recent run (<5, 5-10, 10-18, 18-25, 25+ km), capped at one step above the years level. Levels: none, beginner, novice, intermediate, advanced, elite. A big week never makes a newcomer "advanced".
- Missing data counts at the conservative end. A stated weekly volume is started at 90%.
- After a break of more than 28 days only the last 14 days of data count.
- Safety first: chest pain, fainting, palpitations, unusual breathlessness, or a known heart, diabetes or kidney condition without clearance means no running plan; refer to a doctor. Pain at rest means no running plan.
- Paces come from a race or time trial within 8 weeks (Daniels VDOT); otherwise RPE and talk test until a 3-5 km effort exists. Marathon time is predicted from the half with an exponent by volume (1.06 at 90+ km/week, 1.08, 1.10, 1.12 below 40); for beginners show a range of plus or minus 8%.
- Max heart rate is estimated, never asked; treat heart rate as a guide next to RPE and talk test.
