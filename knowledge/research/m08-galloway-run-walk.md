Galloway run-walk (m08): a permanent method, not only a beginner ladder.
- Three runs a week (Tue, Thu, weekend); other days rest or non-impact cross-training.
- Run:walk ratio from the runner's actual pace for the session (a table); without a pace start with 30 s run : 30 s walk.
- Heat: one row slower (shorter run segments) from 18 C, two rows from 24 C. If a session felt hard (RPE 5 or more on an easy run) shorten the run segment next time.
- Beginners: lengthen the run segment one step after three sessions with RPE 4 or less and no pain.
- Magic mile: warm up 1.6 km run-walk plus 15-20 s accelerations, one mile at hardest even effort, walk 5 min, cool down; first one from week 3 at RPE 7, then every 2-3 weeks. It gives pace.
- Run-walk is an equal-status finish for heavier runners and for a beginner's marathon (with 26+ weeks).
