Returning from injury (p06).
- Stop and see a doctor or physiotherapist for: pain at rest or at night, pain that rises 2 points during a run or makes you limp, groin or deep hip pain, focal bone tenderness, leg numbness or weakness, calf swelling with warmth, or pain lasting more than 14 days.
- Allowed pain while returning: up to 3/10 during a run before clearance (5/10 after), settling by the next morning; morning stiffness above baseline freezes progression.
- Return with the walk-run table on alternate days: 30 s runs and 60 s walks at first, up to 20 minutes continuous, then 30. Each level twice without pain.
- Until weekly volume is half of before the injury: easy running only, no strides, hills or intervals. Then strides, then one new intensity a week.
- Replace missed runs with equal-duration pain-free cross-training. Repeat injury at the same site within a year: physiotherapist first, volume capped at 80% for 8 weeks.
- Calf or Achilles: strength three times a week.
