Pfitzinger-style marathon and half build (m02). Eligible: marathon, intermediate+, 5+ days.
- Volume tiers by planned peak: up to 89 km, 89-113, 113-137, above; do not jump a tier before a full cycle at the lower one; drop a tier if the runner has fewer days than the tier needs.
- Plan length 18 weeks (12 if fewer weeks are left).
- Pace vocabulary: recovery run very easy; general aerobic run at marathon pace + 15-25%; long run and medium-long run at marathon pace + 10-20%, the last quarter may be faster for advanced runners; lactate-threshold run at 45-60 minute race pace.
- Medium-long run 18-24 km two to three days after the long run.
- Long run 26-35 km, never more than 35 km or 180 minutes, and at most 36% of the week; the duration cap binds first for slower runners.
- Quality supports the long run: one threshold session, marathon-pace work inside long runs, no more than the engine's weekly quality cap. A tune-up race 4-6 weeks out.
