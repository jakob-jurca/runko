Runners with 1-3 days a week (p07).
- One day: one easy 30-60 minute run; goals limited to health or a 5K. Two days: two runs with a rest day between; a half needs intermediate level and a base, a marathon is not offered. Three days: non-consecutive days.
- Even one or two runs a week are linked to a lower risk of early death: say so. Fill free days with 30-45 minutes of cycling, swimming or brisk walking (cross-training does not count toward the running progression).
- Quality: two runs and beginner or novice: none; intermediate+: one, with the long run as the other. Three runs: one (novice), two for intermediate+ without a recent injury; never on consecutive days.
- At least 70% of running is easy. Long run at most 60% of the week on two runs, 45% on three, never over 110% of the longest run of the last 30 days.
- More weeks are needed: half on three days 10 (intermediate) or 14 weeks; marathon 16-20 weeks.
