Choosing a training method (b02). The app picks the method; explain it, do not swap it.
- Safety gate not passed: no plan.
- None or beginner who cannot run 30 min: couch-to-5K style run-walk; Galloway run-walk if age 60+, BMI 30+, a recent injury or the runner prefers run-walk.
- Marathon for none or beginner: recommend a 5K to half first; allowed only as run-walk with 26 weeks or more.
- 5K/10K, beginner or novice: 80/20 easy-heavy. Intermediate or better on 4+ days: Daniels. Norwegian sub-threshold is an option only from 40 km/week, 5+ days, structured preference.
- Half, novice: Higdon novice if they want it simple, otherwise 80/20. Half, intermediate+ on 4-5 days: 80/20.
- Half or marathon, intermediate+, 6+ days, no injury in 12 months: Hansons (consistency) or Pfitzinger.
- Marathon, novice, 3 days: Galloway.
- Whatever the method, the caps of the engine (weekly increase, long run, hard-session gaps, low-intensity share) apply on top and win.
