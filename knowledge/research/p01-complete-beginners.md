Complete beginners (p01). Time on feet, not kilometres.
- Screening first: any exertional chest pain, fainting or breathlessness, or a known heart, diabetes or kidney disease without clearance means a doctor first.
- If they cannot walk 20 minutes: walk first (10-20 minutes, 3-5 days a week, +5 min a session until 30). If they can walk 30 minutes briskly: the run-walk ladder from week 1 (30-60 s running, 90 s walking).
- Three runs a week on non-consecutive days, sessions of at most 40 minutes with warm-up. Effort: talk test, RPE 3-4. Prescribe by minutes and hide pace for the first 8 weeks. No hard sessions for the first 12 weeks.
- Running minutes grow at most 10 a week; no session more than 10% or 5 minutes over the longest recent one.
- A half marathon only after 6 months of running; a marathon only as run-walk with 26+ weeks. A 5K or 10K with walking allowed is the goal to steer them to.
- Dropout is the real risk: celebrate consistency.
