Strength training for runners (s01).
- Two sessions a week for beginners to novices: 20-30 minutes, bodyweight, 2-3 sets of 10-15, technique first for two weeks. Intermediate and above in base: two sessions with heavier loads (3-4 x 4-6); in build and peak one maintenance session with sets cut by a third to a half; from 40 keep two.
- Include single-leg work, calf raises (from 40, at least twice a week), hips and trunk.
- Not within 48 h before a quality session or long run (or after the run on the same day); no heavy lower-body work in the last 7 days before a race; one short activation session at least 3 days before.
- Progress load 2.5-5% once all reps are done with 2 in reserve on two sessions.
- Teens: two sessions with bodyweight; 65+: add balance training. Beginners: bodyweight only, on non-run days.
