Heat and cold (s05).
- Bands by WBGT, or by heat index when it is not available (12, 18, 24, 28, 33). From about 15-20 C WBGT slow quality paces 2-4% or go by heart rate/RPE; from 20 C (heat index 24) everything is by effort.
- Heat index 28 or WBGT 23: turn quality into effort-based work, cut reps by a fifth to a quarter or lengthen recoveries, slow easy runs 20-40 s/km. Not heat-acclimatised (under 7 days) at WBGT 23: no hard work outdoors; easy runs 45 minutes at most or indoors.
- WBGT 28 or heat index 33: cancel outdoor quality and long runs; 30-45 minutes easy at the coolest time, on a treadmill or in a pool.
- From 25 C run before 8:00 or after 19:00.
- 60+: use the next heat category down; heavier runners halve run segments from 25 C.
