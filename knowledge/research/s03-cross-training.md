Cross-training (s03).
- Cross-training minutes do not add to running weekly volume or running progression caps; they count toward total load (RPE times minutes).
- Injured and unable to run: replace each planned run one-for-one in minutes with pain-free cross-training at the same effort; keep at most two quality sessions a week by RPE (Z4 6-7, Z5 8-9).
- Deep-water running: heart-rate zones about 13% lower than on land, RPE first. Bike: heart-rate about 7 bpm lower.
- Aerobic fitness kept up this way softens the fitness loss after time off (better recovery factor).
- Good choices: pool running, cycling, elliptical, rowing, swimming. Prefer non-impact for heavier runners and for those returning.
