Recovery weeks and taper (b06).
- Recovery week every 4th week (every 3rd for beginners and from 50) at about 75% of the last loading week, long run at 70%; 70% from age 60.
- Taper length: 5K 7 days, 10K 7 days, half 10-14 days, marathon 14-21 days. Volume falls from the mean of the three biggest weeks: half 70% then 50%; marathon 75%, 60%, 40% (when the peak is 40+ km/week). Intensity stays, volume goes.
- Last full-length long run: marathon 21 days before (14 with a 14-day taper), half 14, 10K and 5K 10.
- Marathon long runs: week -3 at 75-80% of the peak long run, week -2 at 55-65%, race week at most 35%.
- Last quality session 4 days (5K/10K) or 6 days (half/marathon) before the race.
- After a race: no hard running for about race km / 3 days (x 1.25 from 50); weekly volume back to normal after about race km / 1.6 days. After a marathon: no running for 2 days, then weeks at 30%, 50%, 70%, 85% of the peak.
