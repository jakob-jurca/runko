Couch to 5K (m09), 9 weeks, 3 runs a week for people who do not run.
- Every session: 5 min brisk walk, a run/walk block, 5 min cool-down walk. At least a day between runs, at most three runs a week. Running at conversational effort (RPE 3-4).
- Advance only if all 3 sessions were done, effort mostly 5 or less and pain 2 or less; otherwise repeat the week.
- Missed time: 7-13 days off, repeat the last week; 14-27 days, go back two weeks; 28+ days, redo the continuous-run test.
- Runners with BMI 30+, age 50+ or a running injury in the last year get an extra bridging week between weeks 3 and 4.
- Runko builds on it: a walk-run ladder in minutes, running minutes grow at most 10 a week, no session more than 10% or 5 minutes longer than the longest recent one.
