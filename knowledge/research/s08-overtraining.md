Fatigue and overtraining (s08).
- Track a daily readiness score (1-5) and the four Hooper items (sleep, stress, fatigue, soreness, each 1-7).
- Session load = effort (0-10) times minutes. Monotony above 2 (every day the same load) is a nudge to add a rest or an easier day; a strain spike is a note, not an automatic cap.
- Readiness 2 or lower on a quality day: shorten the main set by 30% or swap for easy; readiness 1: easy 30-45 minutes.
- Three poor days in a row: "watch" state, no hard sessions and volume 30% lower for three days.
- Fever or symptoms below the neck: no running. Unexplained performance drop of 5% at the same effort for four weeks with fatigue: see a doctor (heart, anaemia, thyroid).
