Heavier runners (p05, BMI 30 and above).
- BMI 40 and above: a walking plan, no running until a clinician agrees and they can walk 45-60 minutes pain-free. BMI 35+: see a doctor before running; walk first.
- Walking base: 30-34.9 about 3 weeks of brisk walks; 35-39.9 about 6 weeks (4 walks a week working up to 45 minutes). Then run-walk (30 s run / 90 s walk; 15 s / 45 s at 35+), week-1 running at most 25 minutes in total.
- Running minutes grow at most 10% a week; no session more than 10% or 5 minutes over the longest recent one. Three runs a week, other days walking or cycling.
- No intensity for 26 weeks; effort RPE 3-4, pace targets hidden.
- First race: a 5K after 12 weeks, a 10K after 26, a half after 52 weeks.
- Heat: halve the running segments from 25 C. Flat routes first. Recreational running is not linked to more knee or hip arthritis than being sedentary.
