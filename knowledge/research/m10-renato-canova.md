Canova (m10): specific endurance, for experienced runners only (intermediate+, 40+ km/week, 12+ weeks).
- Everything is prescribed as a percentage of race pace: pace at p% = race pace / (p/100). Regeneration runs stay at 80% of race speed or slower.
- Marathon pace from the half: half pace x 1.05 (advanced), 1.07 (intermediate), 1.09 (novice).
- Phases: general (easy plus strides and short hill sprints), fundamental (a long run at 85-90% plus one session at 105-110%), special (a 60-100 minute run at 90% plus a 105-110% session), specific (a big session at 95-105% every 7-10 days, easy days after it).
- It is demanding: Runko uses it only as vocabulary; its own limits on weekly increase, hard-session gaps and low-intensity share decide.
