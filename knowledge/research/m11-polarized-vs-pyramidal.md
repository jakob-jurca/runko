Polarized or pyramidal (m11): how the hard 20% is spread.
- Low intensity = Z1-Z2; moderate = Z3-Z4 at RPE 7 or less; high = above. Measured over 14 days of running time. Strides count as high minutes.
- Polarized: about 80% low, very little moderate, the rest hard (intervals). Pyramidal: mostly low, then moderate, least high (tempo and threshold). Both work; the floor is what matters: never under 75% low (70% on three runs or fewer).
- In Runko: trained 5K-10K runners go polarized (intervals and repetitions) in the last 6-8 weeks; marathon runners stay pyramidal (threshold and marathon-pace work). Beginners and novices stay easy.
- If most easy days feel moderate, they are too fast: slow them down.
