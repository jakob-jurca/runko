Coming back after a break (b07, Daniels). Count days off, not intentions.
- 5 days or fewer: no change to fitness or volume.
- 6-28 days: restart at 50% of the pre-break week, 75% in the second half of the return period, then normal progression. Strides in the second half; quality after the return period.
- 29-56 days: restart at about 33%; easy running (with strides) until volume is back to half, then one quality session a week.
- Over 56 days: restart at 33%; if they cannot run 20 minutes non-stop, use the run-walk ladder.
- Paces are lowered with Daniels' fitness-loss factor (about 0.985 at 10 days, 0.973 at 14, 0.931 at 28, 0.847 at 56, 0.80 at 72+; a little better if they cross-trained).
- After an injury: walk-run table, easy running only until weekly volume is half of before. After childbirth: the Goom walk-run table.
- Missed a session: do not make it up; repeat the week if under 70% was achieved. Fever or symptoms below the neck: no running.
