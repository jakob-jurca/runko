Teenage runners, 15-17 (p08). Runko starts at 15; younger users get a kind "not yet".
- Weekly ceilings: ages 15-16 at most 45 km, 5 runs, long run 75 minutes; 17 at most 60 km, 6 runs, 90 minutes. Two quality sessions a week at most.
- Weekly increase at most 10%, and after two build weeks a lighter one. A growth spurt (2 cm or more in three months): 5%, no new hills or spikes.
- Longest race: 10 km up to 15, a half at 16-17. A marathon or ultra is refused and a half offered.
- One full rest day a week; never weight-loss, calorie or body-composition targets. Hip, groin or knee pain with a limp, chest pain or fainting: stop and see a doctor. Missing periods or unintended weight loss: supportive message and doctor referral.
- Strength twice a week, bodyweight and technique first; 8-10 hours of sleep. No more organised sport hours than age in years.
