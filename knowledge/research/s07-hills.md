Hills (s07).
- Uphill costs about 4% more time per 1% grade (up to 10%); downhill saves about 2% per 1% (up to a cap). On hilly routes (over 20 m of climbing per km) go by time and effort rather than pace. Over 15% grade: power-hike.
- Beginners with under 8 weeks of consistent running: no hill repeats; hilly easy runs with walking on grades over 6%.
- Novice to intermediate in base or build: one hill session a week may replace one quality session.
- Hill sprints: 6-10 x 8-12 s at 6-10% grade with 2-3 minutes' recovery, on an easy day, from novice level up. Not with Achilles or calf problems.
- Heavier runners: flat routes for the first 8 weeks; avoid long downhills.
