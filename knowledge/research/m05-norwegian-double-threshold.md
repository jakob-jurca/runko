Norwegian sub-threshold (m05). Not for none, beginner or novice runners.
- Singles variant: 1 sub-threshold session on under 5 runs a week, 2 on five, 3 on six or more; on under 40 km/week at most 2. Sub-threshold work is 20-25% of weekly time, hard cap 30%.
- Double threshold (two sessions on the same day, 6 or more hours apart, twice a week) only for advanced/elite runners with 100+ km/week and 10+ runs a week. Long reps in the morning (2000 m or 6 minutes), short reps in the evening (1000 m or 400 m), controlled effort (RPE about 6, lactate 2-3.5 mmol/L), threshold volume at most 25% of the week.
- The point is controlled effort, not a race pace; a runner who goes too hard loses the benefit.
- Runko's caps still apply: hard-session gaps, low-intensity floor, weekly increase.
