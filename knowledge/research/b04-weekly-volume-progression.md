Weekly volume progression (b04). Maintenance holds the load; these caps keep any change safe.
- Weekly increase: about 10% for adults under 50, 8% from 50, 5-7% from 60, and never more than 20% for anyone. Where a percentage rounds to nothing the step is a whole kilometre at least.
- The week after a recovery week repeats the last loading week. A recovery week is about 75% of the last loading week (70% from 60).
- No single run longer than 1.10 x the longest run of the last 30 days: that spike matters more than the weekly ratio.
- The long run is capped by DURATION first: 150 minutes (180 for marathon and longer; 150/120 at 60+/70+). Below about 50 km/week it is also capped as a share of the week: 60% on two run days, 45% on three, 45% on four or more under 40 km, 36% from 40 km. Above 50 km/week 36% is only guidance.
- A stated weekly volume starts at 90%; growth never starts from the goal.
- Maintenance: keep volume steady, alternate slightly lighter weeks, no new build.
