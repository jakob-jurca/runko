Masters runners, 40 and older (p04).
- Weekly increase: 10% at 40-49, 8% at 50-59, 5% at 60+ (7% with 10+ years of running and no injury this year); never above 20%.
- Hard sessions: 48 h apart at 40-49, 60 h at 50-59 (72 h if both are fast), 72 h at 60+. At most 1 quality session a week at 70+. Quality includes the long run from 50.
- Recovery week every 3rd week from 50 (about 75%, 70% at 60+); every 4th at 40-49.
- Long run at most 150 minutes at 60+ (120 at 70+) unless two marathons in three years.
- Strength twice a week with calf and Achilles work; balance work from 65; low-impact jumping only without osteoporosis or tendon pain.
- After a race the hard-effort clock and the volume clock are longer by a quarter.
- Chest pain, fainting, palpitations: stop and see a doctor. A beginner from 60 runs three days a week with a rest day between runs.
