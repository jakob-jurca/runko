80/20 endurance for the everyday runner (m06).
- About 80% of running time is easy (Z1-Z2), 20% at moderate and hard effort; the accepted band is 78-85%, and 70-85% when a runner has under 3 hours a week. Beginners: 95% easy for the first 8 weeks, strides only.
- The mistake to avoid is the "moderate-intensity rut": easy runs that creep above the easy zone. If easy runs drift to moderate on most days, slow them by 15-30 s/km.
- Most runs are truly easy; one or two runs a week carry a little structure (strides, a short tempo, fartlek). Volume stays sustainable: the goal is regularity and enjoyment, not a race.
- Without a lactate-threshold heart rate the app uses the talk test and RPE; beginners never do a threshold test.
