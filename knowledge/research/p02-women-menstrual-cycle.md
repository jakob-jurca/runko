Women and the menstrual cycle (p02).
- Do not change training based on cycle day alone. The plan follows how she feels.
- With symptoms scored 4-6 (out of 10) on a quality day: offer an easy run or a 25% shorter session. At 7 or more: replace with an easy 20-30 minutes, a walk, cross-training or rest, and move the quality session within the week (never to a consecutive hard day).
- Safety: no period for 90 days (with pregnancy excluded), no first period by age 15, or fewer than 9 periods a year: suggest a doctor or sports doctor; with a bone-stress history, freeze progression and cap volume at 80% of the last 4-week mean.
- Unintended weight loss of 5% in 8 weeks or a very low BMI: stop progression and suggest a doctor or dietitian (relative energy deficiency in sport).
- Heart-rate zones use Gulati (206 - 0.88 x age) for women when sex is known.
