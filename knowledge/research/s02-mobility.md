Warm-up and mobility (s02).
- Easy run: the first 5-10 minutes easy, no drills. Long run: first 10-15 minutes very easy.
- Threshold session: 15-20 minutes of warm-up (10-15 easy, 5 drills, 3-4 strides of 15-20 s). Intervals or repetitions: 20-25 minutes including 1-2 minutes at session pace.
- Race: 5K/10K 20-25 minutes ending within 10 minutes of the start; half and marathon 10-15 minutes of jogging and 2-3 strides.
- Static stretching is optional: only for runners who like it, after running.
