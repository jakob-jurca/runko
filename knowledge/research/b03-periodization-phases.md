Phases and plan length (b03).
- Minimum plan weeks: 5K 9 weeks from none, 6 otherwise; 10K 8; half 10 (novice) or 8; marathon 16 (novice) or 12. Shorter than that: offer a shorter race, a later date or a completion goal. A strong runner (intermediate+, already at 80% of the needed base) may go shorter with a warning.
- Longer than the useful length (5K 16, 10K 18, half 20, marathon 24 weeks): a base or foundation block comes first.
- Base: at least 85% easy; strides from week 2 (beginners after 5 weeks of continuous running); no quality for none/beginner, at most one light session for novice+.
- Build: quality sessions per level. Peak: at least one race-specific session a week (5K/10K at race effort, half at threshold, marathon long run with 25%+ at marathon pace).
- Recovery week at the end of each load cycle (3:1; 2:1 from 50 or for beginners); none in the two weeks before a marathon taper.
- Never cut the taper below 5 days (5K), 7 (10K), 10 (half), 14 (marathon). A tune-up race replaces that week's quality and is followed by easy days.
