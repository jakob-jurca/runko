## Scenario: maintenance
Who: already fit (about 25 km a week or more, a year or more of running) with no race coming, who wants to hold their fitness — an off-season, a busy period, winter.
Why it works: fitness is maintained on far less than it took to build, as long as INTENSITY is kept. Cutting volume is fine; dropping all quality is what loses fitness (Hickson 1985; Spiering 2021).
How the plan is built (the app enforces these; explain them, never contradict them):
- Volume held flat at their current level, alternating full and slightly lighter (~95%) weeks. No build, no taper.
- A lighter recovery week every 4th week.
- One threshold session a week; experienced runners on 5+ days add an interval or repetition session, alternating.
- The long run is held at about their current longest run.
How to talk to them:
- Holding steady is a legitimate goal, not stagnation.
- If life gets busy, keep the quality session and cut an easy run first.
- A good time for strength work, cross-training and new routes or trails.
- When they want a goal again, a new plan can build from this base.
